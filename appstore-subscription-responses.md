# App Store Connect — Subscription Responses

Draft for the intended iOS submission with premium digital content unavailable to all iOS users, including existing subscribers. See the internal submission check below before sending.

## 1. Who are the users that will use the paid subscriptions in the app?

Yimaru serves English learners, including students, job seekers, working professionals, and exam candidates, primarily in Ethiopia and the Ethiopian diaspora. The iOS app does not provide paid digital subscriptions or access to premium digital courses and practices, including for existing subscribers.

## 2. Where can users purchase the subscriptions that can be accessed in the app?

No subscriptions can be purchased for access within the iOS app. For iOS users, premium learning content is available only as physical hard copies obtained by contacting Yimaru support. These materials are used outside the app, and purchasing them does not unlock premium digital content on iOS.

## 3. What specific types of previously purchased subscriptions can a user access in the app?

None. Previously purchased subscriptions do not unlock premium digital courses or practices in the iOS app.

## 4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?

None. No paid digital content, subscriptions, or premium features are unlocked within the iOS app. When users attempt to access premium content, the app displays: “This content is only available on hard copy. Contact our supports to purchase the physical copy.”

## 5. Can users purchase physical goods or services together with digital content in your app? If so, please describe how the physical and digital content are connected and why you bundle them together in a single purchase.

No. Physical hard copies are obtained separately by contacting Yimaru support and are used outside the app. They are not bundled with premium digital access in the iOS app, and no combined physical-and-digital purchase is offered within the app.
