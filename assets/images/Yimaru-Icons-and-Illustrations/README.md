# Yimaru icons and illustrations

Everything used in the final app design, taken from the same files as the canvas.

## 01-Icons (58 icons)
- `svg/`: brand colors, 24x24 viewBox, grouped by category (navigation, tab bar, audio and media, actions, status and progress, learning content, profile and settings).
- `svg-mono-currentColor/`: single-color icons that take the text color from code, for tinting active/inactive tab states and so on.
- `png@3x/`: 72x72 PNGs for quick use.
- `_preview.png`: the full set with names.

Brand logos are not included. Use the official files instead:
- Google sign-in button: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
- Telegram logo: https://telegram.org/tour/screenshots

## 02-Illustrations (328 SVGs)
Grouped by the canvas sections (1 Landing to 14 Yimi). Each folder has a `_preview.png`.
- Levels, modules and lessons: unique illustration for every one.
- DET, IELTS and English For Professionals lessons.
- `14-brand-yimi-mascot/`: Yimi in every pose, full body.

Colors: plum #8E2483, deep plum #5E1557, yellow #FFD166, coral #FF8A65, mint #2FB67C, blue #5B7FE8, ink #2A1830.
